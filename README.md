With a background in **Mechatronics Engineering**, I approach software testing from first principles—bridging deep backend validation, scalable automation frameworks, and autonomous AI workflows.

- 🏦 **Current Role:** Quality Engineering SPOC at **Geidea** (Merchant Portal & APEX Reconciliation Systems), driving end-to-end quality across high-throughput payment gateways and onboarding architectures.
- 🛡️ **Shift-Left & Architecture:** Focused on early-stage requirement analysis, uncovering business logic gaps before code is written, and validating system-versioned databases, APIs, and distributed logs.
- 🤖 **Applied AI & Agentic Engineering:** Designing AI-driven QA pipelines (cutting manual test design effort by **90%** via **n8n** and custom LLM skills) while actively building production-grade **Agentic AI backends** in **Python & FastAPI**.
